Delete your TallySplit account

In the app

  1. Open TallySplit and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email will.tallysplit@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in, email address, password and phone number.
  • Your profile (name, username and photo).
  • Your notification settings and your phone's notification address.

What stays

So everyone else's balances stay correct, the expenses, payments and receipt photos you added to shared groups stay in those groups, shown as added by "Deleted user". They're no longer linked to you. Crash reports, which never include your name or email, are deleted automatically by our crash-report provider after a limited time.
