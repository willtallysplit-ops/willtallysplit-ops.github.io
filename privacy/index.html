TallySplit

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

TallySplit helps people keep track of shared costs. This page explains what information the app keeps, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't track how you use the app.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, name and username, and, if you add them, a phone number and profile photo. If you sign in with a password, it's stored securely by our database provider and we never see it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • What you add to groups: groups, members, expenses (amounts, descriptions, dates, categories, notes, how they're split), payments, receipt photos, and the activity history of each group.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, an address for your phone so we can send them, and which kinds you've turned on or off.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a report of what went wrong, your phone model, its operating system version and the app version. Crash reports don't include your name, email, IP address, expenses or anything you typed.
  • On your phone: a copy of your groups and any changes or photos waiting to be sent, so the app works offline. Signing out removes this copy.

Who can see it

  • Members of your groups see your name, username and photo, and everything in the groups you share: expenses, payments, receipt photos and the activity history.
  • Other TallySplit users who search for you by username, name or your exact phone number see only your name, username and photo, never your groups or your phone number.
  • Anyone with an invite link to a group can see that group's name, icon, currency and member names before joining, until the link expires (24 hours).

Services we use

These companies process information for us, only to run the app:

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in and photo storage (United States).
  • Google — "Sign in with Google", and delivering notifications to Android phones.
  • Expo — building the app and sending notifications.
  • Sentry — crash reports, without personal information.
  • GitHub — hosts this website and the invite page.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete account). This deletes your sign-in, your profile and your phone's notification address. To keep everyone else's balances correct, the expenses, payments and receipt photos you added to shared groups stay in those groups, shown as added by "Deleted user". Crash reports are deleted automatically by Sentry after a limited time.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, username, phone number and photo in Account → Edit profile.
  • Turn each kind of notification on or off in Account → Notifications.
  • Leave a group at any time; your history in it stays for the other members.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask a question, by writing to will.tallysplit@gmail.com.

Children

TallySplit isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
